REV A - Season 01

BlueSpark AUV

The autonomous underwater platform of KN SPA i BLSPS - first generation, season 2025/2026.

8-thruster BlueROV2 Heavy configuration, full 6 degrees of freedom, NVIDIA Jetson Orin compute stack. Software on ROS 2 Humble with a YOLOv8 perception pipeline augmented by synthetic data from Gaussian Splatting.

[02] Subsystem: Mechanical REF: MECH

Open-frame design from 3D-printed PLA plates - 4 walls joined with steel angle brackets. Central acrylic pressure hull (~500 × 180 mm) houses the electronics. 8-thruster layout in BlueROV2 Heavy configuration: 4 horizontal (45° vector arms) + 4 vertical.

  • Dimensions: 0.65 × 0.57 × 0.34 m
  • Dry mass: ~35 kg, tuned to slightly negative buoyancy (~100-200 g)
  • Thrusters: 8× TD1.2 ESC 12V (~1.2 kgf each), waterproof
  • DOF: 6 (Surge, Sway, Heave, Roll, Pitch, Yaw) - full directional redundancy
  • Materials: PLA frame, ASA payload, removable steel ballast plate
  • Waterproofing: multi-stage sealing - epoxy resin to seal printed parts, Dichtol as secondary pore-penetrating sealant
  • Hull seal: O-rings on end caps + plexiglass dome for the front camera (17 m depth rating)
  • CAD: Onshape -> Siemens NX

Trade-off: open frame over enclosed hull - serviceable, acceptable drag at low speeds. Every part printable on university FDM printers.

[03] Subsystem: Electronics REF: ELEC

Architecture with two independent power systems: internal (main) for propulsion and compute, external (auxiliary) for payload and cameras. Physical separation - a payload fault cannot reach propulsion.

  • Heavy compute: NVIDIA Jetson Orin (autonomy, mission planner)
  • Perception cluster: 4× Raspberry Pi 5 (one per camera)
  • Flight controller: Pixhawk + Holybro baseboard (MAVROS bridge)
  • Microcontrollers: Arduino Nano (sensor I/O), ESP32 (telemetry)
  • Main battery: Tattu 22000 mAh 4S LiPo (14.8 V, inside hull, charged via external connector)
  • Aux battery: 4S LiPo (external, easily removable) - RPi + payload servos
  • Power chain: 2× ANL 300 A fuses → Talamex 300 A kill switch (mechanical, IP67) → bus bars → 2× Holybro PDB-300A → 8× ESC
  • Sensors: Blue Robotics Bar30 (depth I²C), Ping Sonar (altitude UART, 100 m range) + LIDAR (floor distance for ducking), DroneCAN RM3100 compass
  • Hydrophones: 3× Aquarian AS-1 in equilateral triangle + Teensy 4.1 TDOA processor (for Octagon task)
  • Connectors: Weipu WK20 IP68 + Blue Robotics WetLink JPT

Kill switch is mechanical (not MOSFET) - software-independent fail-safe, decision in ADR-006.

[04] Subsystem: Software REF: SOFT

Stack built on ROS 2 Humble + Ubuntu 22.04 LTS, deployment via Docker. Architecture split into 4 functional domains: perception, localisation/control, mission planning, safety. The mission planner runs as a Behavior Tree (py_trees) - reactive, modular, with a readable decision diagram. Key innovation: synthetic data pipeline from 3D Gaussian Splatting addressing the turbidity domain gap in training data.

PKGGaussian Splatting pipeline

Generates photorealistic synthetic training data from multi-view image sequences (COLMAP -> OpenSplat). Wavelength-dependent absorption + scattering simulate varying turbidity. The varied-turbidity condition achieved the highest mAP@0.5 on real-image test data.

PKGCompetition strategy

The vehicle operates in the Search & Rescue role. Primary tasks: Gate, Avoid Debris (Slalom), Recon (Bins). Secondary: Torpedoes, Octagon. The choice maximises reliability over scoring potential for a first competition entry.

  • Language: Python 3 (majority of codebase)
  • Middleware: ROS 2 Humble + colcon, Docker + docker-compose deployment
  • Perception: YOLOv8 + OpenCV, training on PLGrid Athena (NVIDIA A100)
  • Flight stack: Pixhawk running ArduSub firmware (low-level control, attitude/depth hold)
  • Control: MAVROS bridge to Pixhawk (arming, mode, RC override)
  • Mission planner: Behavior Tree (py_trees) - decision in ADR-009
  • Cameras: 4× Raspberry Pi HQ + Arducam LN036 (M12 telephoto 20°), Charuco calibration
  • Repo: github.com/kn-spa-i-blsps/bluespark
[05] Payload REF: PAYLOAD

Two actuator systems for competition tasks: marker dropper (Task III - Bins) and torpedo launcher (Task IV - Torpedoes). Controlled via Adafruit PCA9685 (I²C) in a mineral-oil-filled isolated enclosure.

SYSMarker dropper

Dual-chamber gravity-release, ASA-printed. PowerHD LW-13MG servo (35 kg·cm) rotates the gate disc 90° to release a golf-ball marker (42.67 mm) into the bin. Chambers 40 mm apart for independent targeting.

SYSTorpedo launcher

Dual-tube spring-loaded, ASA-printed with PTFE liner. Steel spring retained by a servo-actuated latch. 45° servo rotation launches the torpedo (lead-shot ballasted, neutral buoyancy). Aiming via vehicle yaw. Spring-loaded over pneumatic: removing the gas bottle simplifies servicing.
