TEST 2025-07-23 · Lab - 1000 l tank

Electronics enclosure leak test

First full pressure test session on the main electronics enclosure. Hermeticity confirmed, seals holding.

Date2025-07-23
LocationLab - 1000 l tank
Durationmin. 10 min hold
ResultPASS

Session goals

  • Validate enclosure hermeticity in a 1000 l tank at ~1 m depth
  • Multi-stage seal verification: epoxy resin (printed parts) -> Dichtol (pore penetration) -> O-rings (end-caps) -> plexiglass dome (front camera)
  • Pressure hold min. 10 min with no detected leak
  • Identify critical points in Weipu WK20 / WetLink JPT cable glands
  • Trial run the disassembly and reassembly procedure

Context

Session ahead of the first electronics integration. Before battery pack, ESCs, main controller and sensors go inside the enclosure, we needed confidence the seals hold at the target operating depth - otherwise the first pool test ends with thousands of zlotys in destroyed electronics.

This is the first validation in the rev A enclosure lifecycle. The assumption is that if rev A passes, future revisions (rev B…) are only optimizations without changes to the sealing approach.

Goals

  1. Global hermeticity - whether the fully assembled enclosure holds the external pressure equivalent to our target operating depth.
  2. O-ring seals - whether the end-cap O-rings hold, whether they were properly silicon-greased, whether there is any deformation.
  3. Cable glands - the highest leak risk on any AUV. Each gland tested individually.
  4. Disassembly procedure - usually skipped in validation, but important: can the enclosure be opened in the field (pool, competition) without special tooling.

Method

Multi-stage seal verification in a 1000 l tank:

  1. Stage 1 - epoxy: printed enclosure parts coated with epoxy resin, vacuum-pulled to remove bubbles, visual validation.
  2. Stage 2 - Dichtol: secondary pore penetration after epoxy cure.
  3. Stage 3 - O-rings: silicon-greased, position checked in end-cap grooves.
  4. Stage 4 - integration: assembled enclosure submerged in the 1000 l tank at ~1 m depth.
  5. Hold: minimum 10 minutes under pressure, leak monitoring through critical points - end-caps, cable glands (Weipu WK20 / WetLink JPT) individually, front camera plexiglass dome, cylindrical joint.
  6. Photo and video documentation of every phase - 8 photos + 3 videos.

The session was static - no dynamic loading. Real conditions (shocks, thruster vibration) will be tested in the wet tests.

Results

Headline result: PASS.

Rev A enclosure passes the hermeticity test in a 1000 l tank at ~1 m depth, hold over 10 min with no leak. All four seal stages (epoxy / Dichtol / O-rings / plexiglass dome) validated.

Design conclusions

  • O-ring sealing architecture confirmed - rev A does not need sealing changes.
  • Multi-stage seal (epoxy + Dichtol + O-ring + dome) is reproducible in the service cycle.

What’s next

  • Electronics integration into the enclosure - next step.
  • Next test after integration: full wet test in the pool with real ballast.
  • Hull architecture ADR (enclosure type, depth rating) - in progress.
