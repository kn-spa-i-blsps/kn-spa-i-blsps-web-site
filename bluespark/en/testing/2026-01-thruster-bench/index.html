TEST 2026-01-20 · Lab

Thruster bench validation

All 8 TD1.2 thrusters operational, ESCs respond to PWM, Pixhawk + MAVROS delivers the correct signal.

Date2026-01-20
LocationLab
Duration4h
ResultPASS

Session goals

  • Individual test of each of the 8 TD1.2 thrusters
  • Bench thrust measurement (no water)
  • Validate ESC + PWM control from Pixhawk 6X
  • Verify PWM range 1100-1900 us

Goal

First systematic thruster test after electronics integration. Before the frame goes into water, we need to confirm that:

  1. Each of the 8 thrusters works individually
  2. The TD1.2 ESC responds to PWM across the full range
  3. The bluespark_control -> MAVROS -> Pixhawk -> ESC loop runs end-to-end

Method

Bench test (no water). Each thruster mounted in a rig with a lab dynamometer where available - otherwise qualitative inspection.

  • Sequential control of each thruster individually via the SetRCOverride service
  • PWM sweep from 1100 us (full reverse) to 1900 us (full forward), step 100 us
  • Logging the response + flagging unusual vibration / sound

Results

PASS. All 8 thrusters respond to PWM, ESCs operational, Pixhawk delivers correct PWM through MAVROS.

Conclusions

  • A torque allocator at the ROS 2 layer must map a 6DOF target onto 8 PWMs - in progress in bluespark_control.
  • Zero-thrust calibration (1500 us == stop) confirmed.
