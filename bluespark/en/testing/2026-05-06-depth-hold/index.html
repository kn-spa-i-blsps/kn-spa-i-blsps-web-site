TEST 2026-05-06 · Pool

Depth hold test - holding the target depth

Depth hold works - ~3-4 cm steady-state drift, recovery to setpoint after a manual perturbation under 3 s.

Date2026-05-06
LocationPool
Duration~3 h
ResultPASS

Session goals

  • Validate the depth hold loop (Bar30 -> Pixhawk EKF -> ArduSub depth control)
  • Measure steady-state depth drift at a fixed setpoint (target: <=5 cm)
  • Recovery time to setpoint after a vertical perturbation (manual push)
  • Stability across setpoint changes (steps: 0.5 m, 1.0 m, 1.5 m)

Goal

Validate the depth hold loop - whether the vehicle can autonomously maintain a target depth without manual correction from the surface station. Critical for every RoboSub task: without a stable depth there is no meaningful perception or maneuvering.

Loop: Bar30 (depth, I²C) → Pixhawk EKF → ArduSub depth control mode → PWM to the 4 vertical thrusters. Setpoint pushed from our ROS 2 node via MAVROS.

Method

  1. Vehicle armed, manual control from the surface station.
  2. Depth setpoint sent via the MAVROS /mavros/cmd/command service (code 84 - depth target).
  3. Three setpoint levels: 0.5 m, 1.0 m, 1.5 m.
  4. At each: measure steady-state drift over 60 s + apply 3 manual perturbations (vertical push from the surface station), measure recovery time.
  5. Logging: timestamp + commanded depth + Bar30 reading + vertical thruster PWM.

Results

PASS.

SetpointSteady driftRecovery after perturbation
0.5 m±3 cm~2 s
1.0 m±3 cm~2.5 s
1.5 m±4 cm~3 s

Drift grows slightly with depth - likely Bar30 sensor noise + minor coupling with the horizontal thrusters (underwater fluid effect).

Conclusions

  • Depth hold is ready as a baseline for the mission planner. We currently use ArduSub default gains - they are sufficient.
  • Next step: validate depth hold combined with yaw control (6DOF test scheduled 2026-05-20).
  • Tuning ArduSub PID gains for our exact vehicle (35 kg, BlueROV2 Heavy geometry) - to do if the wet test reveals stability issues during translation.
