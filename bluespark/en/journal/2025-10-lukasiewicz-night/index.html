Aviation Institute Night - Łukasiewicz Research Network

Prototype showcase at the Łukasiewicz Aviation Institute during their open Research Night.

Aviation Institute Night is an open event run by the Łukasiewicz Research Network. BlueSpark hosted a demo booth - a rare chance to show an AUV in a strongly aviation-leaning environment.

Demo

  • Vehicle prototype on a rig
  • Short pool-test video
  • Q&A with visitors (mostly engineering students from across Poland)

Takeaway

The energy of swapping experience with other teams and research institutions - invaluable. A handful of contacts from WUT later entered our recruitment pipeline.
