Build Progress Log

Journal

A chronological record of project progress. Build sessions, software milestones, construction milestones - everything that happened since the program kicked off.

[01] 2026
  1. 2026-02-12
    SOFT

    Distance estimation - triangle similarity vs SolvePnP

    Two parallel approaches to single-camera distance estimation for gates. First results on calibration data.

[02] 2025 Pre-season
  1. 2025-12-10
    SOFT

    bluespark_control integration - MAVROS bridge

    First working control loop: ROS 2 -> MAVROS -> Pixhawk 6X -> TD1.2 ESC. Arming + RC override in the field.

  2. 2025-10-25
    MILE

    Vehicle showcase for Sławosz Uznański-Wiśniewski

    Demo of the BlueSpark prototype for the Polish ESA astronaut Sławosz Uznański-Wiśniewski.

  3. 2025-10-18
    COMP

    Aviation Institute Night - Łukasiewicz Research Network

    Prototype showcase at the Łukasiewicz Aviation Institute during their open Research Night.

  4. 2025-10-04
    COMP

    NASA Space Apps Challenge

    Participating in NASA's global Space Apps hackathon - 48 hours building on top of the BlueSpark project experience.

  5. 2025-09-15
    COMP

    New Mobility Congress - Katowice

    First public project showcase at an industry conference. BlueSpark stand at the New Mobility Congress in Katowice.

  6. 2025-09-01
    SOFT

    Vision pipeline - first MVP

    Real-time YOLO detection + 3D pose estimation on USB/CSI camera. First swing at perception.

  7. 2025-07-23
    TEST

    First electronics enclosure pressure test

    Main electronics enclosure passed the pressure test. First green PASS on the way to the pool.

  8. 2025-07-14
    BUILD

    Prototype build session

    Frame assembly and first integration of electronic subsystems.
